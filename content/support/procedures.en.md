# Fictional support desk procedures

This is a fictional demonstration corpus. It describes no actual company or customer policy.

## Access request

A requester submits an access request naming the service and business reason. In this fictional workflow, a service owner approves the business need and an administrator applies the access change. A technician records the approval reference before making the change. A viewer may read approved procedures but cannot approve access.

## Restore request

Before a restore, a technician records the affected service and desired restore point. The service owner approves the restore target. The technician tests the backup in an isolated environment and compares the restored content with the expected source identity before use.

## Incident escalation

The first responder records the observed symptom, affected service, start time, and evidence. If the service is unavailable, the responder escalates to the service owner. The incident record must distinguish what was observed from what is only suspected.
