# A small first pilot

## What would we test?

Start with one recurring question category, one approved document or repository set, two to four readers, and ten to twenty real questions written before tuning. Compare how people locate and verify the source with their usual method and with the workspace. Record useful citations, misses, unsupported questions, and time where tasks are comparable.

## What is the first conversation?

A 15-minute conversation identifies a recent question that cost the team time: who asked it, where the team searched, how they checked the result, and what a wrong answer could have affected. No private files or customer tickets are needed for that conversation.

## Is the public demo a customer deployment?

No. It contains product descriptions and fictional procedures only. A private pilot would require agreement on the source set, access, operations, data handling, and payment before loading customer material. No paying customer or measured outside-user result has been established yet.
