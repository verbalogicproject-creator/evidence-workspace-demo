# What Evidence Workspace does

Evidence Workspace is an engineering prototype for finding source passages in a selected set of documents and code. An operator explicitly chooses the source files, builds a versioned snapshot, and lets a reader ask questions against that snapshot. The browser shows relevant excerpts, source locations, and a source-version identifier so a person can inspect the underlying material.

## Why a support team might try it

A technician can ask a recurring question such as “Who approves access requests?” and inspect the relevant procedure instead of relying on a confident but unattributed reply. The proposed benefit is less time spent rediscovering a procedure; no customer time saving has been measured yet.

## What makes a result inspectable

The Ask view shows a bounded excerpt, file location, and a retrieval reason. The source inspector shows its digest and related records. The source map can reveal declared relationships and offers 2D, 3D, and list views. A map position is a layout choice, not evidence that two claims are true or related.

## Who built it

Evidence Workspace is being developed by an independent Jerusalem-based developer. It combines an Atlas reading surface with a separate governed Reasoning Graph component. Their presence in one project does not automatically make every retrieved passage a graph fact or durable memory.
